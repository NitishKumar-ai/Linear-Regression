## Problem Statement
Hospitals and large tech companies are building enterprise AI, leaving independent physical therapy and rehab clinics behind. These clinics bleed cash because they lose thousands in billable outcomes due to lack of verifiable patient compliance data at home.

## Demand Evidence
Currently we only have a strong thesis, but the pain is logically sound: missing out on new Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM) billing codes directly hits the clinic owner's P&L.

## Status Quo
Clinic owners and PTs are tracking patient at-home compliance with paper printouts and fragmented texts.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
The Independent Clinic Owner. The narrowest wedge is capturing time-stamped, authenticated patient check-ins to unlock specific RTM billing codes (e.g., CPT 98975 for setup, CPT 98977 for musculoskeletal monitoring) using the lowest-friction patient interface possible.

## Constraints
Patients won't use heavy 3D apps or complex setups. The clinic owner needs a simple dashboard that doesn't require an Epic/Cerner integration on Day 1.

## Premises
1. The core problem is B2B revenue capture (verifiable data for RTM billing).
2. If we do nothing, the clinic owner leaves thousands of dollars on the table every month.
3. We do NOT need to integrate with massive hospital EHRs on Day 1.
4. The biggest risk is patient adoption friction.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: The Low-Friction SMS Tracker
No app for the patient. A Twilio bot texts a magic link. Clinic owner has a Next.js dashboard.
### Approach B: The Vision-AI Web App
A progressive web app (PWA) using local browser computer vision to count reps and check form.
### Approach C: The "Stealth Hardware" Wedge
A cheap, pre-configured wearable (accelerometer band) that tracks movement automatically.

## Recommended Approach
Approach A: The Low-Friction SMS Tracker. The primary goal right now is to validate the revenue model (willingness to pay) as fast as possible before investing in complex AI or hardware.

## System Architecture & Data Schema
- **Database (Supabase):** `Clinics` (npi, tax_id, timezone), `Patients` (timezone), `EpisodesOfCare` (start_date, end_date), `ComplianceLogs` (utc_timestamp, local_date, is_compliant), `BillingRecords`.
- **Auth:** SMS Webhook authentication. Twilio maps the incoming phone number to the patient ID; no web portal auth needed for patients.
- **Reporting & Workflow:** A "Mark as Billed" workflow that generates billing documentation (PDF/CSV) but tracks the billing state inside the app, giving the clinic owner a satisfying completion state instead of just a raw data dump.

## Open Questions

## Success Criteria
- Patient completes compliance check-in on at least 16 days per 30-day billing cycle (~4-5x per week) to meet CMS RTM reimbursement thresholds.
- Clinic owner successfully bills one RTM code using our data.

## Design & UI/UX Specifications
- **Design System:** Medical trust aesthetic. Inter font, slate text (#1a1a1a) on white background, with Emerald (#10B981) for success states.
- **Clinic Dashboard:** Dense 3-column data table optimized for scanning by billing staff. 
  - **Onboarding Setup:** 1-step config to capture Provider NPI, Clinic Name, and Timezone for compliant PDF generation.
  - **Hierarchy:** 1. Actionable "Ready to Bill" patients. 2. "At Risk" patients (nearing the 16-day threshold). 3. Patient roster.
  - **Empty State:** "Bulk Import via CSV" option for rapid setup. The dashboard is seeded with a "Dummy Patient" (16/16 days complete) so the owner can instantly experience the satisfying "Mark as Billed" flow (Day-1 TTFV).
  - **Patient Handout:** A printable 1-pager explaining the SMS system to hand to patients in-clinic.
  - **Compliance State:** Visual 16-segment linear progress bar, tied to the patient's specific rolling 30-day episode of care.
- **Patient SMS Workflow:** No portal. Patients log compliance by simply replying "Y", "Yes", or "Done" to the daily SMS, completely removing load-time and auth friction.
- **Zero-PHI SMS Copy:** 
  - Welcome / Opt-in: "Hi [Name], [Clinic Name] will be checking your PT progress here daily. Reply YES to confirm."
  - Daily Prompt: "Clinic Update: Did you complete your exercises today? Reply Y or N."
