## Vision

### 10x Check
The 10x vision shifts this from a "dumb SMS tracker" to a full Managed RTM Operations service. Instead of selling a software tool to clinics that puts the administrative burden back on their staff (via CSV exports), we integrate directly with their workflow or EHR, handle the patient enrollment and compliance tracking, and only charge when the clinic gets paid. The product acts as a revenue-operations service rather than a standalone dashboard.

## Strategic Blind Spots (Dual-Voice Synthesis)
1. **RTM Eligibility Risk:** A simple SMS tap may not qualify as a medical device or acceptable data for CMS RTM codes (98975/98977).
2. **Missing Patient Incentive:** The patient does all the work for the clinic's financial benefit. Without pain progression charts or PT feedback, SMS fatigue is inevitable.
3. **Double-Entry Friction:** A standalone dashboard with CSV exports pushes compliance risk and labor back onto the billing manager. The biller/admin is the real operational buyer.
4. **Demand Evidence:** "Clinics lose thousands" is a thesis. We need actual reimbursement dossiers and denial histories before building the full dashboard.
