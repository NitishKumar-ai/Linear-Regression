## Problem Statement
Indian government school education follows a '100-year-old training' model while the world moves toward AI. Teachers are overburdened with 'zero-result' government programs and redundant manual/digital data entry (the double burden). Students read generic AI outputs, causing 'cognitive atrophy' and poor recall (MIT 2025 research).

## Demand Evidence
"I want this product to make govt of India uses this for government school organise classes maintaining their applications data." The user has identified a massive, unorganized 'supply chain' in the school ecosystem where results are currently zero despite policy pushes.

## Status Quo
- **Teachers:** Dual data entry (physical registers + state portals), leading to ~42% time loss on non-teaching duties.
- **Students:** Using generic AI (answer-first) which acts as a 'crutch' rather than a 'scaffold,' leading to poor retention.
- **System:** Broken, clustered market with no organized lifecycle from doubts to jobs.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Target Human:** The Career-Minded Teacher/Principal in a PM SHRI hub school.
- **Narrowest Wedge:** The AI Compliance Assistant + Socratic Tutor. Delete the administrative burden for teachers while using Socratic scaffolding to improve student learning outcomes.

## Constraints
- **Offline-First:** Mandatory due to 'Maintenance Vacuums' and rural connectivity gaps.
- **Multilingual:** Vernacular content is required for universal adoption in Indian schools.
- **Regulatory:** Must align with NEP 2020 Holistic Progress Card (HPC) mandates.

## Premises
1. **The Burden is the Enemy:** EdTech wins by deleting teacher work (automation), not adding it (ERP).
2. **Socratic over Answers:** AI must move from providing answers to Socratic scaffolding to prevent cognitive atrophy.
3. **Offline is Life:** Offline-first sync is a mandatory requirement, not a feature.
4. **Lifecycle ownership:** The ultimate value prop is career support, competitions, and job placement.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Minimal Wedge
- Summary: Focus exclusively on automating teacher data entry and attendance.
- Effort: S
- Risk: Low
- Pros: Instant utility; easy adoption.
- Cons: Doesn't build the student lifecycle.

### Approach B: Ideal Lifecycle (CHOSEN)
- Summary: Build the student lifecycle core today, including doubt-clearing, community events, and career readiness tracking.
- Effort: L/XL
- Risk: Med
- Pros: Realizes the long-term vision; builds a sticky student community.
- Cons: High implementation cost; requires robust data model.

## Recommended Approach
**Approach B: Ideal Lifecycle.** We will build a unified Student Hub that integrates 'Focus', 'Tasks', and 'Study Plans' into a career-readiness timeline. The backend will prioritize PostgreSQL for the relational lifecycle data, using offline-first sync as verified in the current mobile code.

- **Vision:** Successful hosting of the first platform-wide student competition.

## Information Architecture
The Student Hub is organized as a calm, focused workspace with a clear three-tier hierarchy:

### 1. Primary: The Learning Stage (Center)
- Current Active Task or AI Classroom session.
- Prominent 'Focus' status and timer.
- Socratic Assistant sidebar for immediate doubts.

### 2. Secondary: The Ladder (Right/Sidebar)
- Vertical timeline of career readiness milestones.
- 'Next Achievement' nudge.
- Achievement badges and competition highlights.

### 3. Navigation & Utilities (Left/Bottom)
- Quick links to 'Study Arena', 'Tasks', and 'Milestones'.
- Global search for doubts/questions.

  FEATURE              | LOADING | EMPTY | ERROR | SUCCESS | PARTIAL
  ---------------------|---------|-------|-------|---------|--------

  Socratic Assistant   | Pulsing | "Ask a Doubt" | "LLM timeout" | Verified Answer | Follow-up Question
  The Ladder           | Spinner  | "Start Learning" | "No achievements"| Badge Glow | Milestone Tracked

### Empty State Designs
- **The Ladder:** "Your journey starts here. Take your first test to unlock your first milestone." (CTA: 'Browse Tests')
- **Socratic Assistant:** "I'm here to help you think, not just give answers. What's confusing you right now?" (Examples: 'What is an atom?', 'How to solve X?')

## Responsive & Accessibility
The Student Hub is mobile-first, ensuring students can study anywhere.

### 1. Viewport Adaptation
- **MOBILE (<768px):** 
  - Sidebar navigation collapses into a Bottom Tab Bar (Dashboard, Doubts, Ladder).
  - Socratic Assistant is a floating action button (FAB) that opens a full-screen drawer.
  - Focus timer is a persistent sticky header.
- **DESKTOP (>1024px):** 
  - Full three-column layout (IA section).
  - Persistent left nav and right milestone sidebar.

### 2. Accessibility (a11y)
- **Touch Targets:** All clickable items (buttons, tabs, milestone nodes) are 44px minimum.
- **Keyboard Nav:** Full 'Tab' and 'Enter' support for Socratic chat and Focus controls.
- **Contrast:** WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliant (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for body text on cream background).
- **ARIA Landmarks:** `main`, `navigation`, `complementary` (The Ladder), `search`.

## Unresolved Design Decisions
The following ambiguities should be addressed during the initial implementation phase:

  DECISION NEEDED              | IF DEFERRED, WHAT HAPPENS
  -----------------------------|---------------------------
  Achievement Share Visual     | Students can't share wins, reducing virality.
  Offline Sync Indicator       | Confusion when doubts don't appear in web dashboard.
  Socratic Nudge Frequency     | AI might become annoying or too passive.
  Milestone 'Glow' Animation   | Static achievements feel less rewarding.

### Recommendations
1. **Achievement Share:** Generate a "milestone card" (PNG) for social sharing.
2. **Sync Indicator:** Simple status dot in the Socratic Assistant header (Gray: Offline, Green: Synced).
3. **Nudge Frequency:** One nudge per 30 seconds of student inactivity during a doubt session.

## NOT in scope
- **Multilingual JSONB storage:** Explicitly deferred; will use simple text for now.
- **Doubt Archiving:** All data will be kept in the hot path for AI training.
- **Automated Placement Engine:** Focusing on tracking milestones, not automating the placement itself.

## What already exists
