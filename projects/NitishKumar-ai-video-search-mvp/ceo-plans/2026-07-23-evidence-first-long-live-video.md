# CEO Plan: Evidence-first Long and Live Video Search
Generated by /autoplan on 2026-07-23
Branch: feat/milestone-3 | Mode: SELECTIVE EXPANSION
Repo: NitishKumar-ai/video-search-mvp

## Vision

Prove that investigators can find and export defensible evidence from long recordings faster than manual review. Reuse the resulting durable segment-analysis platform for live streams only after pilot demand validates live monitoring.

## Accepted scope

- Real-footage quality corpus and investigator outcome gates
- Evidence provenance and export
- Live security prerequisites before stream registration
- Buy-versus-build live media spike

## Deferred

- Full case management
- Multi-region ingest
- Custom model training
- Search-backend replacement

## Key boundary

Recorded and live orchestration remain separate. They reuse a stateless, idempotent segment-analysis kernel.
