# Test Plan
Generated by /autoplan engineering review on 2026-07-23

Repo: NitishKumar-ai/video-search-mvp

## Affected routes

- Upload lifecycle: create, sign parts, resume, complete, abort, expire
- Source/video progress and retry
- Search across recorded/live/all with authorization and revision filtering
- Playback/HLS and evidence export
- Stream registry, health, start/stop, and SSE updates
- Retention administration and reconciliation

## Critical paths

1. Resume a multipart upload after browser and API restart; verify checksum and dispatch.
2. Search completed early segments while later segments process.
3. Kill a worker at each DB/Qdrant/object commit boundary; resume without duplicates.
4. Prove an unauthorized lexical-only hit cannot cross source grants.
5. Seek to first/middle/final-hour hits in a 12-hour VFR fixture.
6. Export evidence whose manifest hashes, time mapping, revision, and audit reproduce.
7. Disconnect/reconnect RTSP with PTS reset; preserve session/discontinuity playback mapping.
8. Tombstone during playback and legal hold; reconcile every backend deterministically.

## Service-backed suites

Use PostgreSQL, Redis, Qdrant, MinIO, FFmpeg, and MediaMTX fixtures. SQLite and in-memory Qdrant remain unit-test accelerators only.

## Load and chaos gates

- Two-hour and twelve-hour recorded fixtures, same steady-state memory envelope.
- One-stream canary followed by ten 1080p streams at 1 FPS for 24 hours on named hardware.
- Broker, DB, vector store, object storage, disk, worker, decoder, and camera faults.
